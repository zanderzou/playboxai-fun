Read the starting, middle and ending frames of a clip before using it. Playbox's terms describe variable AI-generated output. A useful review checks the movement you asked for and the parts of the image that are meant to stay consistent.

## Look for changes outside the action

Imagine a fully clothed own-photo portrait where you raise a coffee mug. Compare the starting image with the frame where the mug passes the face. Check that the sleeves, collar and background remain suitable. At the end, look for a changed reflection, text label or object you did not intend to show.

## Choose a version with a clear purpose

Share only a clip you have reviewed and are entitled to publish. Keep a private source copy and label the generated version clearly. If a noticeable change affects the person or setting, choose another result or keep the original still.
