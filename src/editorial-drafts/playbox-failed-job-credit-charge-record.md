**A failed task and a credit deduction are two records to reconcile.** One describes what the service reported about processing. The other describes what happened to the balance. Neither should be inferred solely from the other.

Playbox's [payment terms](https://www.playbox.com/terms-and-conditions) are restrictive, including wording about technical issues and consumed credits. The general fee clause also contains a required-by-law qualification. An outside guide should not turn those passages into a guaranteed refund or a universal legal conclusion.

## Preserve the original task state

Keep the task reference, submission time with time zone, displayed status and exact error. Distinguish failed, still processing and completed but inaccessible results. A blank preview does not necessarily identify which state the underlying task reached.

Do not repeat a paid task just to see whether the same error occurs. A new attempt creates a new reference and potentially another charge without explaining the first.

## Match the ledger entry

Record the balance immediately before and after the relevant interval if available, plus the entry associated with the task. Keep purchases, deductions and reversals distinct.

For invented arithmetic, an opening balance of 100, a recorded deduction of 20 and a later recorded reversal of 20 returns to 100. If no reversal is recorded, a support acknowledgment alone should not be labeled a restored balance. These figures are examples, not current Playbox prices or an observed account.

If several tasks exist, do not assign every deduction to the one failed preview without matching references or a provider explanation.

## State the question precisely

A useful inquiry asks whether the identified credit entry corresponds to the identified task and how the provider accounts for its recorded state. If you also request repayment, name that as a separate desired outcome.

The [privacy policy](https://www.playbox.com/privacy-policy) describes support and content handling. A minimal technical and billing record can often explain the issue without sending the underlying private media again. Use the official private support route and remove unrelated personal information from screenshots.

## Keep review, decision and posting separate

Receipt of a ticket does not establish approval of a credit adjustment. Approval does not prove a balance entry has already appeared. Preserve each response and verify the final recorded outcome when one is confirmed.

We did not run a paid task, request a refund or test the billing system. The useful result is a coherent account of one discrepancy, with the published payment limitations and any account-specific decision kept explicit.
